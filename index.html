My journey into technology started in high school and college with a simple curiosity about computers and the possibilities they held. Entering college with very little programming knowledge, I threw myself into learning a wide range of languages and technologies. Along the way, I developed a deep appreciation for clean code and design patterns. Yet, even when I spent most of my days coding, I never fully saw myself as a traditional developer.

Over time, my curiosity began to shift. I realized that clean code and clever patterns are only half the story—what truly fascinated me was what happens after the code is deployed. I became captivated by how a well-thought-out product can step into the real world, solve a genuine struggle, and quietly improve the day-to-day lives of ordinary people.

That realization is how I found my home in Product Management. Today, as AI reshapes the technology landscape, the role of a Product Manager is evolving rapidly—and my technical roots and product mindset have come full circle. In the age of AI, the line between 'planning' and 'building' is disappearing—and the future of tech belongs to Product Builders who can connect clean systems to real human problems.

I carry that builder’s DNA into everything I do. By combining systems thinking, AI-driven workflows, and deep human empathy, I focus on what matters most: bridging the gap between technological possibility and everyday reality—turning technology into meaningful solutions that make people’s lives a little bit easier, better, and more connected.
